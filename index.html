🌐 Needleman-Wunsch Algorithm (Global Alignment)

Goal: Finds the optimal end-to-end alignment between two complete sequences.

  • Initialization: $H(i,0) = i \times d$ and $H(0,j) = j \times d$.
  • Recurrence:
    $H(i,j) = \max \begin{cases} H(i-1, j-1) + s(S_1[i], S_2[j]) & \text{(Diagonal: Match/Mismatch)} \\ H(i-1, j) + d & \text{(Up: Gap in Seq 2 / Insertion)} \\ H(i, j-1) + d & \text{(Left: Gap in Seq 1 / Deletion)} \end{cases}$
  • Traceback: Begins at the bottom-right corner $H(M, N)$ and traces back arrows until reaching $(0,0)$.

🔍 Smith-Waterman Algorithm (Local Alignment)

Goal: Identifies the most highly conserved local subsequence or motif within larger sequences.

  • Initialization: $H(i,0) = 0$ and $H(0,j) = 0$ (all boundary cells are 0).
  • Recurrence:
    $H(i,j) = \max \begin{cases} 0 & \text{(Floor: resets negative scores)} \\ H(i-1, j-1) + s(S_1[i], S_2[j]) \\ H(i-1, j) + d \\ H(i, j-1) + d \end{cases}$
  • Traceback: Starts at the cell with the maximum score in the entire matrix $\max H(i, j)$ and stops when reaching any cell with a score of $0$.

💡 Biological Scoring Conventions:

- Match: Positive reward (e.g. $+1$ or $+2$) for conserved nucleotides/amino acids.
- Mismatch: Negative penalty (e.g. $-1$) representing evolutionary point mutations / substitutions.
- Gap Penalty ($d$): Negative penalty (e.g. $-2$) representing insertions or deletions (indels).
